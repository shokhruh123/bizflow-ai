# BizFlow AI — 3-Minute Winning Pitch (Section 3)

**Presenter script · 10 slides · 180 seconds · English**

> Timing assumes ~15s on slides 1–2 and 9–10, ~30s each on the demo heavy slides mid-pitch.
> The live demo (Slide 3/part of 6) is the knockout punch — practice it until smooth.

---

## Slide 1 — Hook & Title  (0:00–0:15)
**Visual:** BizFlow AI logo, bold word "THE BUSIEST PLACE IN YOUR BUSINESS IS A CHAT — AND YOU ARE IGNORING IT." Small: "BizFlow AI — Conversational Sales Copilot."

**Script:**
"Good evening, judges. Every minute of my next 3 minutes, a shop owner in Tashkent loses a sale — because a customer ordered by chat, and nobody answered in time. We are BizFlow AI. We turn those chat messages into revenue. And I am going to show you a live order being processed in front of you — no script, no screenshots."

## Slide 2 — The Pain (0:15–0:35)
**Visual:** "30%" giant number; a messy chat bubble; icons: Telegram, WhatsApp, voice note, paper note; checklist of human errors.
**Stats (quantified):**
- 90% of SMB customer conversations in Central Asia happen on Telegram/WhatsApp — not on websites or mail.
- Orders arrive unstructured: voice notes, mixed languages (uz/ru/en), missing addresses, typos.
- Result: **~30% of potential revenue lost** — slow responses, wrong items shipped, orders never logged, no follow-up.
- Owners spend **2–4 hours/day** copy-pasting chat orders into Excel and messaging couriers.

**Script:**
"Walk into any grocery, textiles or pharma SME and the 'order system' is a phone and a notebook. Messages pile up in Uzbek, Russian and English — voice notes recorded in a moving car. Someone types them into Excel. Items get mixed, addresses guessed, and a third of the money quietly slips away. The pain is not a software problem — it is an attention problem. And attention is exactly what AI can scale."

## Slide 3 — The Solution & Demo Intro (0:35–1:10)
**Visual:** 3-step pipeline: Messy chat → BizFlow AI (LLM tool calling) → Structured order + invoice + CRM row.
**One-liner:** "BizFlow AI is the sales assistant that never sleeps. It reads any message, any language, any channel — and books the order."

**Live demo (practice 2× per day):**
"Watch. This is a real message sent by a customer on Telegram. Five jars of honey, Chorsu market, a phone number hidden at the end of the sentence. One click — and BizFlow extracts the product, the quantity, the address and the contact, prices it, and generates a branded PDF invoice. Under three seconds, the order is in the dashboard. I will also let the judges send a message in our live chat simulator after the pitch."
